Audience packs to sell

Advertisers buy people, not screens. Package inventory around who is walking past, and price each pack from the segment CPMs above plus a scarcity premium.

BIZ

Business Traveller

Weekday 05:00–09:00 and 16:00–20:00 departures, fast-track lanes, business lounge, gate screens on capital-city routes.

  • Banks, B2B tech, premium auto, law & consulting
  • Sold as share-of-voice, 2- or 4-week cycles
  • Price: segment CPM × 1.2 daypart premium
LSR

Summer Leisure Wave

June–September peak, holiday charters, families and inbound tourists with high retail and F&B intent.

  • Telcos (roaming), car rental, attractions, FMCG, duty-free brands
  • Sold in seasonal blocks with sampling add-ons
  • Price: seasonal index 1.3–1.6 on base CPM
PRM

Premium & Lounge

Lounge, priority boarding and long-haul gates. Smallest audience, highest income and attention.

  • Luxury, watches, private banking, real estate
  • Exclusive category sponsorship, annual
  • Price: fixed fee with audience guarantee
ARR

Welcome / Arrivals

Baggage reclaim and arrivals hall. Short dwell, but the first brand message in the destination.

  • Taxi & ride-hailing apps, hotels, local banks, tourist boards
  • Domination packages and reclaim belt wraps
  • Price: flat monthly with impression floor
LOC

Local Catchment

Meeters, greeters and airport staff. Repeat exposure to the same residents every week.

  • Local retail, auto dealers, employers, utilities
  • Low CPM, high frequency, sold programmatically
  • Price: open-market DOOH floor
DATA

Audience Extension

Wi-Fi and app opt-ins let a campaign follow travellers to mobile and online after they leave the terminal.

  • Any pack above, plus measured footfall or sales lift
  • GDPR consent captured at portal login
  • Price: per opt-in or as a % uplift on the media buy

Pricing logic

One formula prices every audience-driven slot. Fixed formats (static, sponsorship) use rate cards but should be sanity-checked against the same audience value.

FactorWhat it capturesTypical range
Base CPMValue of the segment (income, decision power, travel purpose)€10 – €90
Zone factorDwell and attention: gate lounge > security queue > walkway > reclaim0.7 – 1.4×
Daypart factorBusiness waves, first-wave departures, late charters0.8 – 1.3×
Seasonal indexMonthly traffic relative to the annual average0.4 – 2.1×
Exclusivity premiumCategory lock-out, single-advertiser domination+15 – 40%
Data upliftAudience extension or measured attribution attached+10 – 25%

Slot price = impressions × base CPM / 1000 × zone × daypart × season × (1 + premiums)

Rollout

The order matters: measurement first, because every later price increase depends on being able to prove the audience.

Months 0–3

Measure the audience

Link flight schedules, gate assignments and security throughput to screen logs. Publish an audited impressions-per-zone number.

Months 3–6

Repackage the rate card

Move from per-face pricing to the six audience packs. Add seasonal and daypart indices.

Months 6–12

Open programmatic

Connect DOOH to an SSP with floor prices per segment, to sell unsold time without undercutting direct deals.

Year 2

Add data & retail media

Launch Wi-Fi opt-in audience extension and closed-loop retail-media deals with duty-free and F&B tenants.
