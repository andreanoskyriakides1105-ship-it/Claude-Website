Judge's verdict

No single model wins on every measure. The strongest plan combines them in sequence, using each one where it is strongest.

Year 1 base

Partnerships + rate card

Lock in category partners and naming rights (model D) to cover the minimum guarantee with fixed income before the season starts. Sell the remaining static and digital faces on a clean rate card (model B).

Year 1 upside

Audience pricing on digital

Reprice digital screens by segment and flight wave (model A). Send unsold time programmatically with segment floor prices (model E), so peak summer traffic is priced at peak rates.

Year 2 growth

Data and retail media

Build Wi-Fi consent and closed-loop sales data with duty-free and F&B tenants (model C). It has the highest ceiling but the slowest ramp and the most delivery risk, so it should grow on top of a base that already covers the guarantee.

All defaults are illustrative planning assumptions for a 5M-passenger, summer-peak regional airport. They are not market data or quotes. Replace them with real passenger statistics, inventory and rate cards before using outputs in a proposal.
